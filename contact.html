Frequently Asked Questions

What is the fee structure? ▼

Course fees vary by program. Please call us at 094245 34563 or visit the institute for current fee details. We offer affordable pricing with flexible payment options.

When do new batches start? ▼

New batches start every month. You can join at any time as we accommodate new students regularly. Contact us to know the next batch date.

Yes! We provide placement assistance and career guidance to our students. We have tie-ups with local businesses and organizations for job opportunities.
